AeroDash

Don’t dodge.Graze.

Anyone can leave a gap. AeroDash only pays you when you don’t — slide close enough to scrape the neon and the multiplier climbs. Lose your nerve for two seconds and it resets to one.

The only mechanic that matters

Clean pass, comfortable gap×1
Close enough for the hull to flash×2
Four near misses without breaking the chain×4
Chain held through a full pattern×8

A cautious run and a reckless run cover the same distance. Only one of them scores. That is the whole game, and it is why the leaderboard is not a distance leaderboard.

Nothing to download, ever

There are no asset files in this app. Every ship, obstacle, particle and explosion is drawn in code at runtime, and every sound is synthesised the same way. Nothing is fetched, nothing is streamed, nothing is unpacked on first launch.

The practical consequence is the one you care about: it plays identically in airplane mode, in a tunnel, and on hotel wifi that has given up.

What else is in there

Nine ships to unlock with tokens you earn by playing. A daily streak that remembers you came back. Procedural obstacle patterns, so the hundredth run is not the tenth run memorised. Thirty-four languages, because the menus were written in a string catalogue rather than hard-coded.

What it doesn’t have

No accountNo loginNo energy timer No leaderboard you must be online to seeNo analytics
