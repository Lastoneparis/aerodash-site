AeroDash — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 23, 2026

AeroDash is a neon arcade game designed to work entirely on your device, even in airplane mode.

Data we collect

We run no servers of our own, and AeroDash has no accounts and no sign-up. Your best score, tokens, unlocked ships, daily streak and settings are stored only on your device (and your device's standard backups) — we never receive them.

The one exception is advertising. When ads are shown, Google AdMob receives device and usage data as described under Advertising below. If you buy “Remove Ads”, no ad-related data is processed at all.

Playing offline

The game is fully playable with no internet connection, and no ads are shown while you are offline. With no connection, nothing about how you play leaves your device.

Purchases

The optional "Remove Ads" purchase and token packs are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. These are one-time purchases — AeroDash has no subscriptions.

Advertising

AeroDash shows ads to players who have not bought “Remove Ads”. Ads are served by Google AdMob, a Google service.

When an ad is served, Google may collect device identifiers, coarse location derived from your IP address, and app-usage data in order to deliver and measure that ad. This is described in Google's Privacy Policy and in AdMob's data practices.

On iOS, AeroDash asks for permission to track using Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you decline — or never answer — the device advertising identifier (IDFA) is not used and the ads you see are non-personalized. You can change this at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.

Ads appear on iPhone and iPad only. The macOS, Apple TV and Apple Vision Pro versions of AeroDash serve no ads and contain no advertising SDK.

Buying Remove Ads stops all advertising permanently, after which no ad-related data is processed.

Children

AeroDash is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ad serving follows Google's policies for age-appropriate treatment where they apply. Parents can disable in-app purchases and restrict advertising through iOS Screen Time settings.

Your rights

We hold no account or profile about you, so there is nothing on our side to export or delete. Deleting the app removes all local data. For ad-related choices, see adssettings.google.com, or withdraw tracking permission in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.

Terms of Use

AeroDash uses Apple's standard Licensed Application End User License Agreement (EULA).
